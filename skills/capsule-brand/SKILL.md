# Capsule Security — Brand Guidelines

This skill makes you a brand guardian for Capsule Security. When you're helping with any
creative or communications task, apply these guidelines. When someone asks a brand question,
answer it directly from this reference.

---

## Brand Foundation

### Brand Story

Capsule secures collaboration between AI and humans, providing advanced protection without
disrupting productivity. It ensures safe, seamless interactions — empowering innovation with
confidence and security.

### Brand Personality

**Confident, precise, and trustworthy.** The brand is built on strength and clarity, offering
protection while empowering progress through a forward-thinking aesthetic. Capsule is always
in control, and ensures reliability with every connection.

### Brand Voice

- Calm authority — never boastful, never anxious
- Precise language — say exactly what you mean, nothing more
- Forward-looking — frame everything around enabling the future, not fighting the past
- Empowering — the user is in control; Capsule gives them that power

### Core Brand Statements

- "Calm. Secure. Confident."
- "Secure. Premium. Modern."
- "Built trust — Own the future."
- "Stronger AI security starts with understanding AI"
- "Runtime security for your GenAI"
- "Protect your company's sensitive data and reputation at scale"

### Writing Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**

- Use active, direct sentences
- Lead with benefit, not feature
- Use "you" and "your" — speak to the reader
- Keep sentences short and punchy for headlines; allow more nuance in body copy

**Don't:**

- Use fear-based language or catastrophizing
- Use jargon without context (especially in external-facing copy)
- Use exclamation marks aggressively — confidence doesn't need to shout
- Use buzzwords like "revolutionary", "game-changing", "disruptive"

## Color Palette

### Primary Brand Color

| Name          | HEX       | RGB          | Use                                                |
| ------------- | --------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Aqua Mint** | `#56C8AD` | 86, 200, 173 | Primary brand color — logos, CTAs, key UI elements |

### Secondary Brand Color

| Name          | HEX       | RGB          | Use                                           |
| ------------- | --------- | ------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Neon Mint** | `#16E19A` | 22, 225, 154 | Secondary — accents, hover states, highlights |

### Neutral Colors

| Name           | HEX       | RGB           | Use                                     |
| -------------- | --------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Deep Ocean** | `#002223` | 0, 34, 35     | Dark backgrounds, primary text on light |
| **White**      | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Light backgrounds, text on dark         |
| **Soft Gray**  | `#F0F0F0` | 240, 240, 240 | Secondary backgrounds, dividers         |

### Gradient

**Capsule Deep Teal Gradient** — used for hero backgrounds, large visual elements. Flows from
Deep Ocean `#002223` toward Aqua Mint `#56C8AD`.

### Color Distribution Guidelines

| Color Role              | Share  | Usage                                         |
| ----------------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Neutral Colors          | 60%    | Backgrounds, layout structure, breathing room |
| Brand Color (Aqua Mint) | 25–30% | Brand presence, key visuals                   |
| Secondary Brand Colors  | 5–10%  | CTAs, graphs, highlights needing attention    |
| Text & Iconography      | 10–15% | Typically Deep Ocean or White for clarity     |

### Print — Pantone Equivalents

When sending to print, use Pantone Spot Colours — never convert to CMYK.

- P 130-5 C (Coated)
- P 124-5 U (Uncoated)
- 7480 U
- P 126-16 C (Coated) / P 126-16 U (Uncoated)

Always request a print sample from the shop and confirm they can run Pantone spot inks.

## Typography

### Primary Typeface: Montserrat

Used for all Display and Heading levels.

> "This typeface embodies clarity, modernity, and a touch of technical sophistication — known for its cleanliness, readability, and approachable aesthetic."

**Weights in use:**

- Regular
- Medium
- Semibold

**Usage principles:**

- Headlines: Semibold or Medium, generous line height
- Body / supporting text: Regular
- Never use Bold weight for body — it reads as shouting
- Never mix typefaces; Montserrat handles everything

## Logo

### Logo Variants

| Variant                               | When to use                        |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **Capsule Aqua Mint with Deep Ocean** | Light backgrounds — preferred      |
| **Capsule Aqua Mint with White**      | Dark backgrounds — preferred       |
| **Capsule Deep Ocean**                | Monotone on light background       |
| **Capsule White**                     | Monotone on dark background        |
| **Capsule Black**                     | Monotone, special use              |
| **Capsule Logomark** (icon only)      | App icons, favicons, small lockups |

### Logo Description

The Capsule logo embodies efficiency and innovation. Its sleek design represents forward
momentum, clarity, and reliability.

### Logo Clear Space

Always maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the logomark's capsule
shape. Never crowd the logo with other elements.

### Logo Misuses — Never Do These

- Do not stretch the logo horizontally or vertically
- Do not rotate the logo
- Do not restyle the logo to stroke (outline only)
- Do not add a drop shadow behind the logo
- Do not rearrange elements of the logo's components
- Do not use colors outside of the brand palette
- Do not place the logo on a low-contrast background
- Do not use color combinations outside these guidelines

## Motion & Animation

### Three Core Motion Principles

1. **Control** — All motion must follow a clear hierarchy. No randomness or chaotic movement,
   except when intentionally visualizing the "before Capsule" state.
2. **Trust** — Motion is slow and soft, but rhythm changes are decisive and firm. Safety,
   trust, and confidence.
3. **Less is more** — Motion and effects are intentionally minimal, reinforcing system
   stability. A truly powerful system does not need to prove itself.

### UI Easing — Timing Guidelines

| Type                                 | Duration  | Purpose                        |
| ------------------------------------ | --------- | ------------------------------ |
| Standard (hover/press, icon changes) | 200–280ms | Neutral, balanced              |
| Enter Transitions                    | 250–300ms | Guide attention to new content |
| Exit Transitions                     | 150–200ms | Clear content efficiently      |

### Motion Misuse — Never Do These

- No bouncing or overshoot techniques (no cartoonish feel)
- No chaotic motion except to show the pre-Capsule "before" state
- No fast pacing — slow is intentional; use cut-on-action or match-cut only when controlled

### Logo Animation

Available in Essential and Extended versions for:

- Social media intros/outros
- Presentation openings/closings
- Application launch screens
- Website activation moments

Key technique: **Fading and blur** + **Fading and Gradient** — emphasize softness to build
trust. Gradient color highlights smoothness.

### Hero Animation Concept

- **Before Capsule:** Chaotic, fast stream, dark background, turbulent energy, complex core
- **After Capsule:** Cleaner stream, slower light, brighter background (static dot pattern),
  soft calm core

## Visual Style

### Mood

"A calm, modern visual system built on clarity and control. Soft gradients and deep contrast
express intelligence, trust, and security."

### Material Feel

Mixed glass and metal materials, defined by subtle gradients and controlled lighting — expressing
the balance between transparency and structure.

### Key Visual Elements

- **3D capsule figures** — representing protection, intelligence, and system reliability
- **Energy core** — representing complex information in a calm, readable manner

### Styleboard Aesthetic

- Dark deep-ocean backgrounds with mint/teal accents
- Soft glow effects, not harsh light
- Data visualisations are clean and precise — no chart junk
- UI screenshots show a polished, premium dark-mode interface

## How to Apply These Guidelines

### When creating a presentation

- Background: Deep Ocean `#002223` or White `#FFFFFF`
- Headlines: Montserrat Semibold, Aqua Mint or White
- Body text: Montserrat Regular, White or Soft Gray
- Accent/highlight: Aqua Mint `#56C8AD` or Neon Mint `#16E19A`
- Use the Deep Teal Gradient sparingly on hero slides
- Refer to slide deck samples in the brand book for layout patterns

### When writing marketing copy

- Open with a calm, confident statement — not a question or fear hook
- Use the brand voice: precise, empowering, forward-looking
- Keep headlines under 8 words when possible
- Use "AI security", "AI protection", "GenAI security" — not "AI defense" or "AI fighting"

### When designing social media content

- Use templates from the Google Drive social media folder as a base
- Aqua Mint is always the dominant brand color in the composition
- Deep Ocean background is the default — white backgrounds are acceptable for LinkedIn
- The logomark or full logo always appears; never leave a post unbranded

### When building UI / product features

- Follow the color distribution: 60% neutral, 25–30% brand, 5–10% secondary
- Motion: subtle, controlled, never bouncing — use the UI easing table above
- Typography: Montserrat throughout; no substitute fonts
- Icons: line-style, clean, consistent weight

### When checking if something is on-brand

Ask: Is it calm? Is it precise? Does it feel in control? If yes — it's on brand.
If it feels chaotic, noisy, or uncertain — it's off brand.
